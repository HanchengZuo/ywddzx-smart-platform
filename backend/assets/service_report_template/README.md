# Service report template

User-supplied source: 9月业务督导中心现场服务巡检报告（基本情况）.pptx.
The source contains 13 pages at 4:3. The renderer adapts native geometry to
16:9, replaces only the specified variables, removes obsolete photo relationships
and notes/comments, and embeds new chart workbooks instead of external Excel links.
Page 13 remains the supplied explanatory risk matrix.

Chapter two follows pages 14-35 of the user-supplied full deck
9月业务督导中心现场服务巡检报告.pptx, especially the two-column layout on page 17.
The editable chapter is recreated with native shapes and contain-fit evidence photos,
ordered by business units and paginated from real category/stage content, not example data.

The last five editable pages follow full-deck pages 36-40: two section dividers,
two common-issue summary pages, and three recommendations in a rounded dash-dot frame.
Only validated evidence-grounded AI text is substituted; no example findings are reused.

Branding is shared with all reports through report_branding.py. The original
petroleum wordmarks are replaced by assets/operations_center_logo.png (user-supplied
operations-center logo), on the cover and all subsequent headers. The renderer
preserves its intrinsic aspect ratio after adapting the full deck to 16:9.
